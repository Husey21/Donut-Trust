Safer Transactions

Middleman Service.

For high-value trades, Donut Trust's rules require the use of a middleman. Keep the process visible, agreed, and documented.

01

Open a request

Go to #middleman-request in the official Discord server and provide trade details.

02

Wait for staff confirmation

Use only a middleman explicitly confirmed by the server's staff.

03

Confirm the terms

Both parties should agree on the item, amount, sequence, and conditions.

04

Complete and document

Follow the middleman's instructions, confirm completion, and post a vouch.

Proof of Completed Trades

Vouches & Reputation.

A vouch is a public record that a trade went well. Keep feedback honest, specific, and tied to a completed transaction.

How to post a vouch

Use @DonutSMP Vouch Bot with the /vouch command, or follow the format below in the dedicated channel.

@username
Trade: [what was traded]
Amount: [value]
Notes: [optional]

Vouch requirements

Only post vouches for completed trades. Both parties can vouch each other. Transaction screenshots are strongly encouraged. Fake vouches can result in a permanent ban.
